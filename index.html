packd

Eight friends voted yes on the trip poll. Then someone had to book the villa with their own money. Packd makes a yes cost something, so the organiser never fronts the trip.

    Payments are simulated. The money hold uses UPI Reserve Pay, which lets a bank block an amount now and debit it later, the same way IPO applications block funds.
